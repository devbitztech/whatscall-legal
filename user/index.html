Privacy Policy — WhatsCall

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatsCall ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the WhatsCall mobile application.

Contact: dev.bitztech@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Phone number and/or email address, verified via one-time password (OTP)
  • First name, last name
  • Profile photo (selected from your device camera or gallery)
  • Referral code (if applicable)
  • Device ID (used for guest sign-in and account/device association)

1.2 Chat and Call Data

  • Content of messages you send within the App, stored to enable conversation history
  • Call metadata: call type (audio/video), call status, start/connect/end timestamps, and duration
  • We do not record or store the audio/video content of your calls

1.3 Payment and Wallet Information

  • Coin balance and transaction history within the App
  • Payments are processed by our third-party payment processor, Cashfree. We do not store your full card, UPI, or bank credentials — these are handled directly by Cashfree in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable payment security standards
  • We retain transaction records (amount, coins purchased, transaction ID, status) for accounting and support purposes

1.4 Permissions

  • Camera: used to take photos for your profile picture or to share in chat
  • Microphone: used to enable audio during voice and video calls
  • Photos/Media/Gallery: used to let you select and upload images for your profile picture or to share in chat

1.5 Device Information

  • Android device identifier, used for app functionality such as guest login
  • Data stored locally on your device to support app performance and offline access to app state

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide chat and call functionality between you and hosts on the platform
  • Process coin purchases and maintain your wallet balance
  • Verify your identity via OTP
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App

3. How We Share Your Information

  • Payment processor: Payment details necessary to complete a transaction are shared with Cashfree
  • Hosts: Your profile information (name, photo) and messages/calls are visible to the hosts you interact with on the platform
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required to comply with legal, regulatory, or security obligations
  • Aggregated Data Recipients: We may share non-identifiable, aggregated insights (e.g. overall usage trends) for internal research purposes
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of WhatsCall, our users, or the public

4. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in jurisdictions with varying data protection laws, including where our servers or service providers (such as Cashfree) operate. We implement reasonable safeguards to ensure your data continues to receive an adequate standard of protection wherever it is processed.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your information. However, no system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users should take precautions to safeguard their account credentials (e.g. OTP codes).

6. User Rights & Preferences

You have the right to:

  • Access, Update, or Delete Your Data: Manage your personal information via the App's profile/settings, or by contacting us
  • Opt-Out of Notifications: Disable push notifications via your device settings

For any privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, contact us at dev.bitztech@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy (Under 18 Policy)

Age Restriction Notice: WhatsCall is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 years and older. Children under 18 years of age are strictly prohibited from using, registering, or creating an account on this application.

WhatsCall is not intended for users or children under the age of 18. If we discover that a minor's data has been collected, we will promptly remove it and terminate the account. See Section 9 (Child Safety Policy) for further detail.

8. Data Retention Policy

We retain your account, chat, call, and transaction data for as long as your account remains active. If an account is deleted, most associated data will be removed unless retention is required to comply with legal or accounting obligations. You can request account deletion via the App's settings or by contacting us.

9. Child Safety Policy

WhatsCall is firmly committed to protecting minors and preventing any form of child exploitation, abuse, or sexualization on its platform. Users violating these rules will face permanent suspension and may be reported to law enforcement.

a. Age Requirement

  • WhatsCall is strictly for users aged 18 years or older.
  • Misrepresenting age or enabling minors to access the App is prohibited.
  • We monitor for underage use and child sexual exploitation material (CSEM) through moderation and, where applicable, detection systems.

b. Prohibited Conduct and Minor-Related Content

WhatsCall strictly prohibits any activities or content involving minors that could lead to exploitation, abuse, or endangerment. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Creating, sharing, or promoting materials depicting or implying sexual exploitation or abuse of minors;
  • Featuring minors in photos, videos, or any other uploaded content;
  • Depicting or suggesting minors in sexualized contexts, such as suggestive poses or clothing;
  • Encouraging or engaging in acts of physical harm, coercion, psychological manipulation, neglect, trafficking, or any form of child endangerment;
  • Creating or managing accounts on behalf of minors, or attempting to falsify age or identity to bypass restrictions.

Any violations of these rules will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to law enforcement.

c. Reporting and Enforcement

  • Suspected child exploitation is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or other relevant authorities.
  • Users may report incidents via in-app tools or email: dev.bitztech@gmail.com.
  • WhatsCall will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

d. Prevention and Awareness

  • This policy is displayed clearly within WhatsCall.
  • Safety reminders are provided during account creation and content sharing.
  • We continuously work to improve moderation and detection to maintain platform integrity.

e. Global Reporting Resources

If you encounter potential child exploitation outside of WhatsCall, please contact:

  • NCMEC (U.S., Canada, Australia, New Zealand)
  • Local police authorities (Europe)
  • ICMEC Hotline (Global)
